    Meet the Leadership Team

    At Camp Lindenmere, we have developed a reputation for providing a nurturing environment led by our Directors and Senior Leadership Team. We are 100% family-owned, and take a personal approach to each individual camper, as we come together as one Camp Lindenmere family.

    Mitch Garfinkel

    Owner/Director

    Mitch fulfilled his lifelong dream of owning and directing a children’s summer camp when he purchased Lindenmere in 2006. Born and raised in Jericho, NY, Mitch graduated SUNY Albany before becoming an attorney. While practicing law and owning several restaurants has been a great experience, summer camp is Mitch’s true passion. His camping experience has taken him through the ranks of camper, CIT, Counselor, Color War General, Group Leader and now Camp Director. His hands-on leadership and daily blogs to parents during the summer help each parent fully understand what is going on at camp and what their children are experiencing. Although his favorite part of the year is spending the summers in Henryville, PA, when not at camp, Mitch lives in Colorado.

    Hayley (Garfinkel) Eisbrouch

    Owner/Director

    Hayley could not be more excited for her 21st summer at her home away from home. She began as a camper in 2007, with her twin siblings Lynsey and Kyle, when her dad, Mitch, fulfilled his lifelong dream of owning and directing a summer camp. Hayley then continued on to be a bunk counselor, Color War General, a Division Leader for 6 years with our youngest girls, an Assistant Director and now a Director. Hayley’s passion for working with children and families as well as her positive energy, warmth and leadership at camp makes her an integral part of our team. She loves spending her days with our campers, watching them smile through all of the best moments of camp that she remembers experiencing back when she was a camper. Before working year-round at camp, Hayley taught Pre-K at the 92nd Street Y in NYC. She has a Master’s Degree in Child Development from Vanderbilt University and a Bachelor’s Degree in Early Childhood Development. Hayley’s passion for camp and youth development continues outside of camp, where she sits on the Associate Board of SCOPE (Summer Camp Opportunities Promote Education). SCOPE is an organization that provides summer camp opportunities to all children, regardless of socio-economic background. When not at camp, Hayley resides in New York City with her husband, Andrew, and their son, Miles.

    Kyle Garfinkel

    Owner/Director

    Kyle returns to camp for his 21st summer, alongside his dad, Mitch, and sister, Hayley. After 8 years as a camper, Kyle transitioned to bunk counselor. He later became the Athletic Director, followed by taking on the roles of Middle Boys Division Leader and later Assistant Director. Kyle has enjoyed building so many relationships with our camp families and keeping in close communication with our parents throughout the summer. Additionally, Kyle oversees our team to operate and run the day-to-day of camp life. The absolute highlight of Kyle’s days during the summer is playing around on Boys Side with all of our campers. His diverse experience and ability to relate to campers and counselors alike makes him an asset to leading our team. He excitedly takes on all aspects of camp as a Director, to ensure that all our campers and their families have the absolute best camp experience. Kyle graduated from Penn State University with a Bachelor’s degree in Hospitality Management. Kyle currently resides in New York City, and his passion for camp continues outside of Lindenmere, where he sits on the Associate Board of SCOPE (Summer Camp Opportunities Promote Education). SCOPE is an organization that provides summer camp opportunities to all children, regardless of socio-economic background.

    Wendy Berliner

    Director

    Wendy looks forward to another wonderful summer with the Camp Lindenmere Family as one of our Directors. She grew up in New York and spent 20+ summers at an overnight camp in Maine. Wendy loves connecting with campers, their families, staff, and alumni. Additionally, she has spent many years of her camping career hiring, onboarding and guiding camp’s staff team to ensure that all of our campers have supportive and caring summer role models. A graduate of Brandeis University, Wendy is also a recovering lawyer, who clerked for the First Circuit Court of Appeals and practiced law at a large, international law firm, before returning to her true love – CAMP! Her best job is being the Soccer Mom to her very busy teenagers. Wendy now lives in a Boston suburb with her husband, Marc, and those very busy teenagers, Rachel and Jacob.

    Elliott “EDog” Roberts

    Director

    Elliott returns to Camp Lindenmere for his 13th summer, now as a Director. Hailing from Australia, Elliott began his camp journey as a Flying Trapeze and Circus counselor in 2011, followed by Head of Flying Trapeze, and soon transitioning into an Assistant Director and Head of Boy’s Side. No stranger to shyness, Elliott will be easy to identify in his brightly color coordinated outfits. It was at camp that Elliott first met his wife, Kayla, and in 2016, camp was also where he proposed to her. When not at camp, Elliott and Kayla love to ride bikes around the beaches of South Florida and spend evening sunsets with their lovable dog, Luna.

    Lewis Gardner

    Assistant Director

    Lewis is excited to return to Camp Lindenmere for his 7th summer—and his 13th year in camping! Originally from the UK, Lewis makes the journey across the pond each summer to the Poconos, where he helps create unforgettable events and experiences for our campers.

    With a background working with visa agencies before setting up his own company in the UK, Lewis now plays a key role in staff recruitment and marketing, while also developing innovative programs that bring fresh energy to camp each year.

    A passionate soccer player and dedicated gym enthusiast, Lewis has recently earned the title of Lindenmere’s resident pickleball expert. Whether he’s on the mic or on stage, you’ll always find Lewis bringing energy, creativity, and fun to camp life.

    Marnie Lang

    Assistant Director

    Marnie is looking forward to her second summer at Lindenmere as an Assistant Director. She brings over ten years of experience creating meaningful, joyful, summer experiences for kids and staff alike. She holds a Master’s Degree in School and Mental Health Counseling from the University of Pennsylvania, grounding her work in a deep understanding of youth development and community wellness. Since her first summer at camp, Marnie knew she wanted to make camp her full-time career and is ecstatic that her dream is becoming a reality at Lindenmere. Her favorite camp memory is a special one: getting engaged at the very camp where her husband grew up. She is excited to make new memories at Lindenmere alongside her two children, Dylan and Sydney, who will be joining her for their own summers of discovery and fun. Marnie’s favorite parts of camp are Visual Arts, Horseback Riding and, of course, S’mores! In the off-season, she lives in New Jersey with her husband, Noah, their kids, and their rescue dog, Lillie.

    Lisa Kelly

    Chief Financial Officer

    Lisa Kelly is the Chief Financial Officer for Camp Lindenmere, after joining the Camp Lindenmere family in 2019. She grew up on the Island of Jamaica, but migrated to the USA over 29 years ago. In 1996, while living in Florida, Lisa met and began working with the Garfinkel family. Lisa fondly recalls how they became a part of her extended family, and credits the realization of her American dream, in part to their love and support over the years.

    Lisa has experience working with some of the largest CPA firms in the State of Florida, and is valuable team member at Camp Lindenmere. Her greatest satisfaction is the opportunity to watch her three granddaughters enjoy the camp life!

    Justin Harkness

    Facility Director

    Justin takes on the role of Facility Director in his second year at Camp Lindenmere. He is a Florida native and UCF graduate who fell in love with camping in 2007. Working nearly 15 summers at a camp in Maine, he taught wakeboarding and waterskiing at an all-girls sleep away camp. It was during these summers that he discovered his interest in working with children and young adults in the action-packed and fun fueled days that make up camping. The community, culture, and creative elements of sleep away camp were a compelling enough argument for him to ultimately leave his year-round job as an estate manager on the main line of Philadelphia in pursuit of a full-time camp position.

    As an angler, hobbyist woodworker, and general outdoorsman, the Poconos are a perfect fit to combine work and play. You can find Justin most days fixing, building, and maintaining the robust list of buildings and features found all around Camp Lindenmere. He is often times assisted by his loyal helper Juniper, his Golden Retriever, who likes camping for the pets and treats! When not holding a tool in his hand, you just might spot him in an afternoon frisbee game tossing a disc around.
