# Camp Lindenmere competitor sweep, Poconos and NE Pennsylvania

Date: November 5, 2026. Companion data file: `competitors-2026-11-05.csv` (27 rows, one per camp plus a Lindenmere reference row, with source URLs per row).

Scope: private sleepaway camps in Wayne, Pike, Monroe and Susquehanna counties that draw NY, NJ and CT families. 24 operating camps were verified on their own websites. Camp Lohikan is included because it is closing. Camp Netimus is included with gaps because its site blocked every fetch. Camp Watonka (closed 2019), Camp Nock-A-Mixon (Bucks County) and Camp Saginaw (Chester County) were dropped as outside the region. Camp Pocono Trails is a weight-loss camp and was dropped as a different category.

## Headline findings

1. Camp Lohikan (Lake Como, coed, one of the largest camps in the area) ran its final season in summer 2026. Its homepage is now a letter from the director saying the property was sold to Camp Morasha and pointing families to Lake Greeley Camp and Camp Cayuga. Those displaced families are the single biggest open opportunity for 2027 enrollment in the region.
2. Island Lake Camp (Starrucca) is Lindenmere's closest price-and-structure twin. Its 2027 sessions are June 27 to July 25 at $9,950, July 25 to August 15 at $8,200 and full summer $15,000, within $100 of Lindenmere on every line and on identical dates.
3. Early-enrollment pricing is standard. Fourteen of the 24 camps publish a dated price break (priority rate, early bird, early enrollment credit or pay-in-full credit). Lindenmere's dates page shows none.
4. The Greeley family of camps (Pine Forest, Timber Tops, Lake Owego) and the Buynak camps (Lake Greeley, Cayuga) sell priced overnight trial stays for first-timers. Timber Tops charges $2,600 for a one-week "Rookie to Ready" trial. Lake Owego charges $5,600 for 12 days. Lake Greeley charges $1,700 for a 7-day Rookie Week. Pine Forest charges $200 for a two-day Explorer overnight and credits it to tuition. Lindenmere's Rookie Days are day visits only.
5. Director home visits are a published offer at Weequahic (with a fall road-show schedule by region) and Canadensis (every new camper gets one). Towanda publishes "New Camper Video Chats."
6. Content cadence: Weequahic (Oct 30, 2026), Equinunk and Blue Ridge (Oct 27), Lake Owego (Oct 9), Pine Forest (Sep 10), Timber Tops (Sep 10) and Chen-A-Wanda (Aug 14) have blog posts from this fall or summer. Lindenmere's News page shows no dated posts.

## A. Directories and advisor services

- ACA Find a Camp (find.acacamps.org): every camp in the sweep has a profile. All are listed as Accredited Member except Camp Netimus (listed as not currently accredited) and ISTC (program listed accredited, camp profile listed not accredited). Cost: ACA's membership page states camp dues start at $395 and scale with budget, and camps seeking accreditation use a separate fee table starting at $700 (acacamps.org/node/37757, per search snippet; the page blocks fetch and 2026 figures are not confirmed).
- KidsCamps.com: Premier listing $899 per year, Standard $599, Basic $149, Entry $99, plus a free listing (kidscamps.com/market-your-camp.camp). Camp Cayuga has a listing.
- MySummerCamps.com: free basic listing; premium tiers "as little as $2.00 per day" (Bronze, Silver, Gold). Towanda, Westmont, Susquehannock and ISTC have listings.
- CampNavigator: site returned HTTP 503 on every attempt; pricing not verified.
- Camp Ratingz (campratingz.com): review site "operated by Shopclerk AI" with a "Submit a camp" link; no pricing shown.
- Tips on Trips and Camps: free to families; FAQ states "directors pay us a referral fee if you choose their program"; represents 600+ programs; runs Zoom camp fairs. Fee amount not published.
- The Camp Connection (Long Island, Westchester, Florida offices): free to families; a sponsored article states every partner camp "pays an identical fee for camper placement." Amount not published.
- The Camp Experts: site did not render; trademark record describes free consultation to families; camp fee not published.
- Camp fairs, 2026 dates found: New York Family Ultimate Camp Fairs (Queens Feb 7, Upper East Side Mar 1, Westchester Mar 7, Brooklyn Mar 8, Long Island and Staten Island Mar 15); ACA-listed Long Island Camp Fair at Hofstra Feb 1, Westchester at Purchase College Jan 27, Millburn-Short Hills NJ Jan 24. ## B. Where Lindenmere sits on price per week

Full summer: $2,143 per week, tied with Island Lake at 9th cheapest of 23 priced camps. Cheaper camps are the budget tier (Moshava, Lake Greeley, Cayuga, Susquehannock, Shohola, Akiba) plus Chen-A-Wanda and Westmont. Every premium coed camp (Towanda, Canadensis, Pine Forest family, Wayne, Equinunk and Blue Ridge, Starlight, IHC, Trail's End, Tyler Hill) is $2,171 to $2,711 per week.

4-week: $2,463 per week, mid-pack among first-half options. Chen-A-Wanda's first-year 4-week is $2,450. Island Lake is $2,488. Towanda's 4-week intro is $2,718. Wayne Boys is $2,900. Pine Forest's half is $3,043. The budget camps are $1,725 to $1,925.

3-week: $2,717 per week versus Weequahic $2,700, Island Lake $2,733 and Shohola $2,067. Only four camps in the region sell a 3-week session.

Deposit: Lindenmere's $2,000 / $3,000 is mid-range (Tyler Hill and Trail's End $6,000; Wayne Girls $3,000; Pine Forest family $2,700; Chen-A-Wanda and Akiba $2,500; Island Lake $1,500 / $2,500; Shohola $1,500; Susquehannock and Moshava $1,000).

## C. Things competitors show that Lindenmere's site does not

1. A dated early-enrollment price break. 14 camps publish one, most tied to a deadline between July 31 and December 31, 2026. Chen-A-Wanda stacks early bird ($300) and pay-in-full ($500). Tyler Hill and Trail's End give $500 for enrolling within 72 hours of a camp visit. Lindenmere shows only the sibling and Leadership discounts.
2. A priced overnight trial for first-timers. Timber Tops (1 week, $2,600), Lake Owego (12 days, $5,600), Lake Greeley (7 days, $1,700), Pine Forest (2 days, $200 credited), Bryn Mawr, Trail's End and IHC all sell or offer an overnight taste. Lindenmere's Rookie Days are day visits only, with no overnight option and no shortest stay under 3 weeks.
3. A first-time camper guarantee or credit. Cayuga and Lake Greeley refund prorated tuition if a first-timer leaves early. Susquehannock gives a $500 first-time credit. Akiba offers a lifetime rate guarantee. Lindenmere has none.
4. Director home visits and regional meetups. Weequahic publishes fall home-visit dates for NY Tri-State, NYC and Long Island, DC, Florida and California. Canadensis visits every new camper at home and runs a spring new-camper event and June open house. Equinunk and Blue Ridge host gaga and Socceroof meetups in the city. Towanda runs video chats, a fall BBQ and winter reunions. Lindenmere's site offers tours and a "Speak with a Director" link but no home-visit, video-call, open-house or off-season meetup offer.
5. Visible content cadence and a named bus route list. Six competitors have blog posts dated fall or summer 2026; Lindenmere's News page shows no dated posts. Tyler Hill (NYC, Paramus, Long Island), Starlight (NYC, Long Island, Westchester, N and S NJ, Philly, Baltimore) and Akiba list specific stops; Lindenmere's dates page says only "NY, NJ, MD, and PA."
