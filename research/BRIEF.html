Camp Lohikan closed. Its families need a camp.

3,600
searches a month for "Camp Lohikan", against 2,690 for Lindenmere

Verified on lohikan.com: 2026 was its final season, the property sold to an Orthodox camp, and the director pointed families to two cheaper camps. These are NY and NJ families already paying Poconos prices.

Build a "for Lohikan families" page and buy Google ads on searches for "Camp Lohikan" this month. The ad text cannot use the name. Post in their parent groups.

A new camper pays back fast. Google alone will not fill the camp.

$3,400 to $9,000
profit from one camper's first summer, after food, staff and supplies

Google search brings in a camper for about $450 to $1,350. Worth running. But only 6,000 to 8,000 people a month in NY, NJ, CT and PA search for a sleepaway camp at all.

Any way of finding a camper that costs under $3,000 is worth paying for. Budget Google as a top-up, not the plan.

Rivals close with a deadline. We do not.

14 of 24
rival camp sites show a dated sign-up discount

Pine Forest $300 off by Dec 31. Tyler Hill and Trail's End $500 off within 72 hours of a visit. Many sell a paid trial weekend credited to tuition (Pine Forest $200). Cayuga and Greeley refund a first-timer who leaves early. Two publish director home-visit calendars.

The owner decides by December 1: one dated discount, one paid trial weekend, one first-timer refund promise.

January through March is more than double the fall. The June and July spike is mostly current families and day-camp overflow, not new prospects. November is the quietest and cheapest month.

Build and test in November and December. Spend in January through March.

Cheap places to start

Ranked by likely cost per new camper, from the Google data and the 24 rival sites. Each is a page on the website first and an ad second.

  1. Lohikan families. One-winter window, highest intent in the region.
  2. Activity pages. Basketball, soccer, volleyball, lacrosse, tennis, horseback, theater, trapeze. Half the cost per click of place-name searches, and no Poconos camp buys them.
  3. A cost guide. "What sleepaway camp costs in 2027" with real tuition and the sibling discount. Rivals hide prices. 320 people a month ask if camp is tax deductible and nobody advertises on it.
  4. Youngest-camper pages. Searches for 6 and 7 year olds have no advertisers. Lindenmere takes 6-year-olds; most rivals start at 7 or 8.
  5. "Co-ed" in every headline. $1.13 a click against $3 to $6 for place names, and several big Poconos camps are single-gender.
  6. The packing list page. Already on Google's first page for twenty searches. Add "see 2027 dates" and an email sign-up. Costs nothing.

Do 1 and 6 this month. Do 2 and 3 before January.

Seven numbers to get from the camp office

These turn the estimates in this brief into Lindenmere's real numbers.

  1. Share of 2026 campers who also came in 2025.
  2. Beds filled versus capacity in 2026, by session.
  3. Average tuition per camper and how many pick 3, 4 or 7 weeks.
  4. Daily food, staff and supply cost per camper.
  5. 2026 new campers by source: referral, advisor, website, Rookie Days, ads.
  6. The current referral credit, and any past sign-up discount.
  7. Past ads: average cost per click, click rate, which states, top-spending keywords, and whether banner ads on other websites and non-Google partner sites were switched on. They are on by default and waste money.

Get these before spending a dollar.
